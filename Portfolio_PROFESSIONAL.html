Automatic Condensate Drainage System

Internship Capstone Ethiopian Airlines Team of 3

Problem Statement: Industrial steam dryer machines at Ethiopian Airlines' laundry facility experienced severe vibration and mechanical stress during startup cycles. Root cause analysis revealed residual condensate accumulation in steam coils, causing water hammer phenomenon when fresh steam was introduced—a critical issue requiring immediate intervention.

Operational Impact: The existing manual drainage procedure relied on operator memory and inconsistent execution, resulting in equipment downtime, reduced operational efficiency, increased maintenance costs, and risk of catastrophic component failure.

Engineering Solution

  • Designed relay-based electrical control system utilizing inverted logic architecture for safe operation
  • Integrated dual-level conductivity sensors for precise moisture detection within drainage conduit
  • Implemented 24V solenoid valve actuation powered by 220V→24V step-down transformer with galvanic isolation
  • Engineered fail-safe design ensuring valve remains closed upon power loss, preventing unintended drainage
  • Achieved response time of less than 2 seconds from moisture detection to complete drainage
  • Reduced system cost by 70-80% compared to commercial condensate management solutions
